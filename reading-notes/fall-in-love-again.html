Books That Made Me Fall in Love with Love

Some books don’t just tell love stories — they remind you why believing in love is worth it.

• The Song Of Achilles — Madeline Miller

• Circe — Madeline Miller

• Galatea — Madeline Miller

Why it hit: the tenderness, the beautiful prose will leave you sobbing, craving for more. Literally anything Madeline writes is a piece of art.

• Heartstopper — Alice Oseman

Why it hit: Super cute characters, found family done so right you'd never get enough of it.

• It Happened One Summer — Tessa Bailey

Why it hit: Romance peaked here, characters you'll end up falling in love with.
