About Me

Hi! I’m Aditya Yadav — an obsessive reader.

I’ve always been in love with books: their addictive smell, the worlds they hold, and the stories that linger long after the last page.

I write because I couldn’t help it. I write emotionally driven romance across contemporary, fantasy, and literary spaces — drawn to longing, intimacy, and the kind of moments that leave a mark.

Outside of writing, I'm a medical student. I’m usually working on multiple projects at once, thinking too hard about character arcs, or convincing myself that starting another book is a reasonable idea.
